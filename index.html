🔗 Linking with Google Forms & Google Sheets

You can connect this app directly to a Google Form and Google Sheets backend. Here are the 2 recommended enterprise approaches:

Option A: Direct Submissions to Google Sheets via Webhook (Recommended)

When participants submit this custom branded form, it posts the data directly to your Google Sheet using a free Google Apps Script web app.

How It Works:

Method 1: Direct Real-Time Sync (Already Configured!)
Your Web App URL is already linked into this app. As long as your Google Apps Script has function doPost(e) saved and deployed with Access: Anyone, every participant who hits "Submit" will instantly insert a new row in your Google Sheet.

Method 2: Zero Hassle — Load Google Sheet CSV (Instant!)
If you collected responses via your own Google Form or Google Sheet:

  1. In your Google Sheet, click File → Download → Comma Separated Values (.csv).
  2. In this Dashboard, click "📂 Load Google Sheet CSV".
  3. All charts, KPIs, daily breakdowns, and qualitative feedback will instantly refresh and become printable!
